Practical 2: Data manipulation

This practical uses synthetic Hospital Episode Statistics (HES) data on hip fractures to practise the core dplyr verbs. It has two parts:

  • Part 1 (Session 2): choosing columns and rows, and sorting, with select(), filter() and arrange().
  • Part 2 (start of Session 3): creating and recoding columns with mutate(), if_else(), na_if() and case_when().

We’ll use the same data again in Session 3, when we combine it with other tables.

All data in this practical is simulated for teaching purposes.

Datasets

hes_admissions

One row per hospital spell. A spell is one continuous hospital stay, from admission to discharge.

  • spell_id, patient_id
  • Admission and discharge dates
  • Admission method and source
  • Discharge destination
  • Hospital (provider_code) and specialty
  • Length of stay in days (los_days)

hes_patients

One row per patient.

  • patient_id
  • Sex
  • Date of birth
  • Area deprivation (IMD quintile, where 1 is the most deprived)
  • Ethnicity
  • GP practice and region

Import

Download hes_admissions.csv and hes_patients.csv and save them in the folder you’re working in. Then load the tidyverse and import both files with read_csv():

library(tidyverse)

admissions <- read_csv("hes_admissions.csv")
patients <- read_csv("hes_patients.csv")

  1. Clean discharge_destination: “UNK” and “Unknown” both mean the destination was not recorded.

  1. Group length of stay into three bands with case_when():
    • “Up to 7 days”
    • “7 to 14 days” (more than 7, up to 14)
    • “Over 14 days”
    Count the number of spells in each band.
TipHint

Conditions in case_when() are checked in order, and each row gets the value from the first condition that is TRUE.

NoteSolution 
admissions |>
  mutate(
    los_band = case_when(
      los_days <= 7 ~ "Up to 7 days",
      los_days <= 14 ~ "7 to 14 days",
      los_days > 14 ~ "Over 14 days"
    )
  ) |>
  count(los_band)
# A tibble: 3 × 2
  los_band         n
  <chr>        <int>
1 7 to 14 days  4817
2 Over 14 days  1574
3 Up to 7 days   320

There are 320 spells up to 7 days, 4,817 between 7 and 14 days, and 1,574 over 14 days. Because the conditions are checked in order, the second condition (los_days <= 14) only applies to stays longer than 7 days.

Note that count() lists the bands in alphabetical order, not in order of length of stay.

  1. Recode admission_method (a HES code) into a new column, admission_route:
    • 21, an emergency admission via A&E, becomes “A&E”
    • 22, an emergency admission via a GP, becomes “GP”
    Use count() to check that every code has been recoded.
NoteSolution 
admissions |>
  mutate(
    admission_route = case_when(
      admission_method == 21 ~ "A&E",
      admission_method == 22 ~ "GP"
    )
  ) |>
  count(admission_method, admission_route)
# A tibble: 2 × 3
  admission_method admission_route     n
             <dbl> <chr>           <int>
1               21 A&E              4838
2               22 GP               1873

Counting both the old and new columns together is a quick way to check a recode: 4,838 spells were via A&E and 1,873 via a GP, with no NA values left over. Because there are only two codes, if_else(admission_method == 21, "A&E", "GP") would also work, but case_when() leaves any unexpected codes as NA, where you’ll notice them.
